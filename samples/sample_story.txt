TIFU by accidentally becoming the local legend of a small mountain town.

This happened about three years ago when I went on a solo hiking trip in a remote forest area. I was staying at a small cabin near a quiet mountain town. 

One evening, I decided to go for a night walk along a well-marked trail with a high-powered flashlight I had bought recently. What I didn't know was that the town's local folklore mentioned a glowing mysterious light on that exact mountain that only appears once every decade.

As I walked up the trail, toggling the light off and on to check my compass, I noticed several cars parked at the lookout point down below. People were pointing upwards and taking photos. I thought they were looking at stars, so I flashed my light three times toward them as a friendly greeting.

The next morning, I went to the local diner for breakfast. The local newspaper headline read: "The Mountain Beacon Returns: Three Flash Warning Signalled to Witnesses."

Turns out, everyone thought I was a mythical mountain spirit. I quietly paid for my coffee, checked out of my cabin, and never brought that flashlight on a night hike again.
